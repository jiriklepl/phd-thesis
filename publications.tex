The \contF symbol indicates a full or major contribution (e.g., leading the implementation of the approach or evaluating the majority of the experiments), \contP indicates a partial contribution (contributing significantly to the work, but not leading it), and \contN indicates no or negligible contribution (e.g., providing feedback on the work, but not directly contributing to the main ideas, implementation, experiments, or writing). The relative contributions are categorized into four aspects: analysis (identifying the problem, formulating the research questions, and designing the approach), implementation (e.g., developing the software artifacts), evaluation (designing and performing experiments, analyzing results), and writing (drafting the manuscript).
