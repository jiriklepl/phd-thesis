\begin{XXX}
This chapter is just an overview of Noarr and Cellato abstractions.
\end{XXX}

Our work on abstractions for high-performance computing introduced layout-agnostic and traversal-agnostic abstractions that enable separation of concerns between data representation, data traversal, and algorithm implementation. We have developed two main abstractions: Noarr~\cite{vsmelko2022astute,klepl2024pure,klepl2024abstractions,klepl2025layout} and Cellato~\cite{brabec2025cellato} as experimental testbeds for exploring and showcasing these ideas.
