
\chapwithtoc{List of Publications}\label{chap:publist}

The table below lists the works that are part of this thesis, along with the venues (conferences, workshops, or journals) where they were published or submitted; journals are indicated by boldface. The works are listed in the order of their appearance in the thesis text, which also roughly corresponds to the chronological order of the venue editions. All publications included in this thesis are peer-reviewed, except for the last manuscript, which is currently under review. The full list of references to these works is provided at the end of this chapter, followed by copies of the corresponding manuscripts (\cref{contrib:vsmelko2022astute,contrib:klepl2024pure,contrib:klepl2024abstractions,contrib:klepl2025layout,contrib:brabec2025cellato,contrib:brabec2025tutoring,contrib:klepl2026shifting}) in the order they are listed in the table.

The \contF symbol indicates a full or major contribution (e.g., leading the implementation of the approach or evaluating the majority of the experiments), \contP indicates a partial contribution (contributing significantly to the work, but not leading it), and \contN indicates no or negligible contribution (e.g., providing feedback on the work, but not directly contributing to the main ideas, implementation, experiments, or writing). The relative contributions are categorized into four aspects: analysis (identifying the problem, formulating the research questions, and designing the approach), implementation (e.g., developing the software artifacts), evaluation (designing and performing experiments, analyzing results), and writing (drafting the manuscript).
