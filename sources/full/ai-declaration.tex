\chapwithtoc{Declaration of Generative AI Usage}\label{ai-declaration}

During the preparation of this work, the author used GitHub Copilot inline suggestions (in Visual Studio Code) when drafting initial versions of the text. The author reviewed and edited all the generated text as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the thesis.

OpenAI's Codex environment (with GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6 models) was used to provide suggestions for text improvements, and Grammarly was used to check for grammar and style issues. All suggestions were reviewed and incorporated by the author manually.
