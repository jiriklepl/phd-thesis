The \contF symbol indicates a full or major contribution (e.g., leading the design and implementation of the main ideas, performing the majority of the experiments, and driving the main conclusions), \contP indicates a partial contribution (e.g., contributing to the work in a significant way, but not leading it), and \contN indicates no or negligible contribution (e.g., providing feedback to the work, but not contributing to the main ideas, implementation, experiments, or writing). The contributions are categorized into four aspects: analysis (identifying the problem, formulating the research questions, and designing the approach), implementation (developing the software artifacts), evaluation (designing and performing experiments, analyzing results), and writing (drafting and revising the manuscript).
